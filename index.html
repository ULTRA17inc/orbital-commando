01 // THE UPLINK

COMMAND FROM ORBIT

Orbital Commando is a 2D side-on war sandbox. You're a commander in orbit, wired into a body on the ground.

Fight with it, buy reinforcements and drop them in by dropship, build out your base and call down orbital strikes. When your body dies, jump into the next soldier and keep going. Lose the uplink and you lose the war.

02 // THE GROUND

EVERY PIXEL
IS REAL

The ground is a simulated material world. Dig tunnels, blast craters, burn the grass and collapse bunkers onto the people inside them.

Bodies take real wounds. They bleed, break and ragdoll, and the battlefield remembers every fight.

  • Destructible soil, stone and concrete
  • Wounds, ragdolls and gibs
  • Calibre-based penetration and suppression
  • Fire that spreads through the grass

03 // FIELD MANUAL

WHAT YOU'LL DO

ORBITAL STRIKES

Aim napalm, mine pods and bunker busters from orbit and watch the ground come apart.

BUILD MODE

Add rooms, defences and generators to your base while the battle is still running.

SQUADS AND RANKS

Order squads, promote veterans and bring medics. Switch between your soldiers at any moment.

DEFEND THE UPLINK

Each side has one. Damage it and their strikes and drops get weaker. Blow it out and they're finished.

LOCAL CO-OP

Plug in an Xbox controller for player 2 and play in split screen.

ENDLESS SKIRMISH

Reroll any map with random traits: caves, gold, weather, time of day and outposts. Or switch the aliens off for a straight two-army fight.
