01 // THE UPLINK

COMMAND FROM ORBIT

Orbital Commando is a 2D side-on war sandbox. You're a commander in orbit, wired into a body on the ground.

Fight with it, buy reinforcements and drop them in by dropship, build out your base and call down orbital strikes. When your body dies, jump into the next soldier and keep going. Lose the uplink and you lose the war.

02 // THE GROUND

EVERY PIXEL
IS REAL

The ground is a simulated material world. Dig tunnels, blast craters, burn the grass and collapse bunkers onto the people inside them.

Bodies take real wounds. They bleed, break and ragdoll, and the battlefield remembers every fight.

  • Destructible soil, stone and concrete
  • Wounds, ragdolls and gibs
  • Calibre-based penetration and suppression
  • Fire that spreads through the grass

04 // HOW IT WORKS

GAME SYSTEMS

The rules under the chaos. Numbers are the defaults from the current build.

THE UPLINK CORE

Every base is built around a vault holding an Uplink Core, the reactor column that links you to your ship in orbit. Its health is your signal strength, in 10% steps.

  • A weak signal makes dropship orders cost more, up to double
  • Strike recharge and dropship hold times stretch, up to 2.5×
  • Base power drops by the same share as the signal
  • At zero, a 5 second SHUTDOWN PROTOCOL counts down, then the core goes up like a mini nuke and you lose
  • You also lose if you run out of both bodies and gold

GOLD

Gold pays for everything: troops, crates, vehicles, base parts and strikes. A base income ticks in all game long, and you earn the rest in the field.

  • Each outpost you hold pays +1.5 gold a second
  • A powered gold extractor in your base pays +2 a second
  • Gold lies underground: every pixel you dig out is worth 0.5
  • Dig up a wreck with the digger to salvage up to 30% of what the vehicle cost
  • Aliens carry bounties: Stalker 50, Bombardier 120, Bone Worm 450, the Nest Heart 2,000

BUY MENU AND DROPSHIPS

Press B to fill a dropship and send it down. Each side has one ship in the air at a time. It lands at your base or at any outpost you hold, and every soldier gets an order for when they land: follow, guard, take an outpost or assault.

  • Up to 4 troops (Commandos) or 5 (Raiders), 3 crates and a walker slung underneath
  • Every delivery has a 50 gold fee
  • Ships have 420 health and can be shot down. The wreck crashes and burns
  • Buggies, rocket bikes and the Kestrel are built at your base instead
  • If your last body dies, a replacement Rifleman is flown in for the usual price plus the fee

BODIES, WOUNDS AND RANKS

You are never one soldier. Hold Q and let go over any of your troops to jump into them.

  • Damage lands on the head, torso, arms or legs. Crippled legs limp and crippled arms shake your aim
  • Wear a limb down and the wound goes to the bone, then the limb comes off
  • Soldiers bleed. Downed soldiers crawl and can still shoot, and a medic can get them back up
  • The jetpack holds 1.5 seconds of fuel: tap for a hop, hold to fly
  • Six inventory slots. Drag to swap, drag out to drop
  • V bashes and F kicks. A bash from behind kills outright
  • Hold Alt to pick a squad and give it orders. On the Tab map you can order squads and AI-crewed vehicles, and save groups to Ctrl+1 to 5

BALLISTICS

Every round is simulated. Rounds slow down and drop over distance, and what they go through depends on the calibre.

  • Rifle rounds punch through dirt, sand and wood
  • Sniper and heavy rounds go through walls
  • Only heavy rounds pierce steel
  • Near misses suppress: the AI ducks and aims worse, and your own view shakes

ORBITAL STRIKES

Press O and a red laser from your ship shows where the strike will land and how far it reaches. Click to fire, or right-click to hold it for later. You get one strike at a time, then it recharges.

  • Bunker busters bore up to 6 metres down and go off in the first room they find. They can't be aimed over a core
  • Enemy strikes give you 4 seconds of warning
  • The AI starts calling strikes from its third wave

THE GROUND

The world is made of real materials, simulated pixel by pixel: topsoil, dirt, sand, snow, rock, concrete, metal, wood, glass, scrap, gold and bedrock.

  • Under the grass: 1.5 m of topsoil, 1.5 m of packed dirt, then a 1.5 m stone layer with gaps in it
  • Gold is only found underground
  • Loose dirt pours like sand. Unsupported ledges, roofs and slabs creak, then collapse into rubble
  • Grass and shrubs burn, and fire spreads
  • The dead stay where they fall. Bodies rot to bones over about five minutes, then sink into the ground

THE ENEMY

The commander AI plays by your rules. It buys troops, builds base parts, makes buggies and bikes, calls in strikes and sends waves that grow as the game goes on.

  • The neutral alien nest under the Frontier sleeps until something disturbs it, then raids both sides
  • Both commanders send hunters after the nest's Heart for its 2,000 gold bounty
  • The Bone Collectors drag rotting bodies back to their pit on the crag
  • Kill a Bone Collector and it leaves acid and a larva. With GIANT WORMS on, the larva becomes a cocoon, then a Bone Worm

05 // FORTIFY

BASE BUILDING

Your base is a bunker dug into the ground around the uplink. Press N to grow it while the battle carries on around you.

  1. 01Pick a part. The camera roams free while your soldier holds still.
  2. 02Place the ghost. It shows green where it fits, and red with the reason where it doesn't: "Too far from the base", "Needs solid ground under it".
  3. 03Snap it on. Rooms and corridors snap onto the bunker and cut their own doorways through. Defences stand on solid ground nearby.
  4. 04Pay and build. You pay up front. The part rises row by row and can be shot while it goes up. Short on power, it takes twice as long.
  5. 05Change your mind. X sells the part under the cursor for half its price.

POWER

The main generator gives 10 units and the starter bunker uses 6, so you begin with 4 spare. Most parts draw power. Go over budget and parts go dark, newest first. A generator room adds 8 more.

CUT THE POWER

Shoot out a base's generator and it goes dark. Doors fail open and turrets sag. After 30 seconds a backup kicks in at half power. A weak uplink signal drains power too.

6 / 10 USED

OUTPOSTS

HOLD THE GROUND

Stand on an outpost unopposed for 8 seconds to take it. It pays 1.5 gold a second and your dropships can land there.

A bunker under every outpost heals and resupplies whoever holds it. Its vendor sells weapons, explosives and med kits straight into your inventory.

HANGAR

THE MOTOR POOL

Every base has a hangar out front, with roller doors, a gantry crane and a repair workshop. Buggies, bikes and the Kestrel roll out from here. Vehicles drive through built parts, but soldiers, rounds and blasts don't.

FACTION STYLE

TWO WAYS TO BUILD

Commando bases are clean concrete and steel under a radar dish. Raider forts are welded scrap with spikes and tyre walls. Inside, every room is fitted out for its job, from bunks to medical beds.
